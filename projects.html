Estrogen

Estrogen is a mod for Minecraft designed around expressing yourself, fluid handling and discovery. Mod is still currently in active development, with careful integration of game design, community and proper principles.
It has accumulating a great community around itself spinning off many more associated projects and engagement.

The mod has been designed around modularity, allowing anyone to create simple to add-on modules, including an official one below.

Create: Estrogen

Create: Estrogen is a module for the Estrogen mod adding compatibility and custom mechanics all based around the Estrogen mod and the ever-so-popular Create mod.

It's designed around seamless integration of both mods, taking advantage of APIs and mechanics within the two mods.

Modrinth • Curseforge • Github

Cynosure

A Library mod in active development constructed around taking full advantage of Kotlin to abstract and implement on top of major Mod Loaders. Allowing for great multi loader development featuring a custom model and animation format, modular event api and a networking abstraction.

In Addition to being a useful library mod, it also serves as playground to experiment with new features and concepts, allowing for fast adoption of new ideas and technologies.
